Kimen / quiet tool-first directions

Kimen is a local-first secrets tool.

Kimen stores secrets in an encrypted local vault and lets you project them into runtime only when you explicitly ask. These three variants all stay in that frame: local vault, ordinary config, prepared env, simple app.
