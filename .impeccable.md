## Design Context

### Users

Kimen is for security-minded developers, infrastructure engineers, and small technical teams who want to keep secrets local-first. They need a practical way to store encrypted secrets, project them only at runtime, and avoid ambient exposure through global shell exports, copied `.env` files, or overly broad agent/tool access.

### Brand Personality

Precise, self-possessed, and quietly radical. The interface should feel confident, lucid, and operational rather than salesy. It should communicate control and restraint instead of hype.

### Aesthetic Direction

Editorial field manual rather than standard SaaS landing page. Light mode first. Warm paper neutrals, mineral greens, rust-red accents, and sharp dark ink. Use asymmetry, section rails, and specimen-like code layouts. Avoid generic feature-card grids, generic startup gradients, or glowing cyber aesthetics.

### Design Principles

1. Show the product through concrete runtime examples, not abstract marketing claims.
2. Make explicit projection and bounded exposure the visual and narrative spine of the page.
3. Prefer asymmetric structure, strong typography, and deliberate negative space over templated card systems.
4. Keep copy terse, operational, and specific to developers and operators.
5. Use motion sparingly for reveal and sequencing, never as decoration.
